# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Settings has a Decode Resolution: automatic, full, half or quarter, automatic by default. Automatic starts every composition and every 3D output change at full resolution and steps down one scale once the shown frame rate stays under 95 percent of the composition's rate, or frames keep dropping undecoded, for 3 seconds. For 2 seconds after a load or a step, and until the player has timed the decode at the new scale, drops and a low frame rate do not count, since the lookahead is refilling. It steps back up once the player reports decode capacity of at least 2.2 times the rate for 10 seconds, never to a scale whose measured capacity fell short of the rate, and never more than one step every 3 seconds. Until the player reports its capacity, automatic only steps down. The player's controls show the resolution when it is not full.
- Settings has a 3D Output for stereoscopic DCPs: left eye, right eye, side by side or top and bottom, applied at startup and on Save. The two both-eye outputs decode twice the pictures. While a 3D source plays, the player's controls show the output in use, and clicking it steps to the next one until the setting changes or the app restarts.
- Settings has "Require HDCP for encrypted content", off by default and covered by the settings lock. On Linux, with it on, an encrypted composition plays from the library or a playlist only when the display output the player window is on reads Content Protection Enabled, and playback stops within 2 seconds of the output leaving Enabled. Otherwise the play is refused naming the output and its state, or saying that no output matched. A playlist row refused this way is skipped. Unencrypted content is never checked. Other systems show the setting as not supported and never block playback.
- Playlist composition rows take an in and an out point, typed as HH:MM:SS:FF timecode at the composition's frame rate or set with Set from the frame on screen while the playlist plays that row. The row plays from the in frame and stops before the out frame, the next composition takes over there with no gap, and the view shows the length of the range. A range past the end of the composition is a warning on the Playlist view. Playlists are saved in format 2, and a format 1 playlist opens as before.
- Settings fills every Player field when the sound devices cannot be listed, and says why under Sound Device.
- Settings has a Monitor Profile for the player: an ICC file picked with the file picker, or one of the profiles colord has for the full screen display, with Clear going back to the built-in sRGB. DCP pictures are shown through it from startup and from Save. A profile the player cannot use is not saved, and the reason, naming the file, shows under the field.
- A version file plays from the library and from a playlist with the library package holding its original version. A version file whose original version is not in the library is refused, naming the assets no package holds.
- Playlist view: Add to playlist on a library row puts the composition in the open playlist, and rows can be moved, removed, given a start time and mixed with intermissions of black or a still image. Each row shows when it is expected to start, and a start time the row before runs past or a composition missing from the library shows as a warning. Playlists are saved by name in `playlists/` in the data directory, and Play or Play from here runs the playlist in the player window. Consecutive compositions play with no gap between them, an intermission or a start time still ahead holds black or the still until it ends, each encrypted composition takes the KDM valid when it loads, and a composition that cannot play is skipped with the reason on the Playlist view. The player's controls show the row playing and the next one with its countdown, and during an intermission or a wait they show the time left in place of the scrubber. Playlists stay open while the settings are locked.
- Settings can be locked with a password, so the player, display, sound and key settings, the library folders and the KDM store stay as the operator left them. While locked, Settings shows only a password field, and saving settings, adding or removing a KDM and removing a package from the library are refused. Unlocking lasts until Lock or the app exits, and a wrong password holds off the next attempt for 5 seconds. The password is stored as an Argon2id hash.
- Settings has a Player section for DCP playback: brightness, masks on each edge, scaling (fit, fill or native), the sound device and layout, an A/V delay, a subtitle offset and a subtitle colour override. They apply at startup and on Save, to a composition already playing too, and a missing sound device or a layout the device lacks shows on the status bar while it holds.
- GPU decoding: Settings has the grok accelerator plugin switch, licence and registration URL the wizards have. The setting applies at startup and on save, and a plugin that refuses to start turns it off with the reason in the status bar.
- Playback opens a player window of its own, as a normal window, while the transport and the library stay on the main window. Moving the pointer over the picture shows controls over it: play/pause, the scrubber with time, Stop and full screen. They fade out while playing, taking the pointer with them in full screen, and stay while paused or hovered. F, a double click or the full screen button go full screen on the display chosen in Settings, Escape leaves it, Space pauses, and Stop or closing the player window ends playback.
- Library, Keys, Jobs and Settings views: packages found under the library folders play in the player window, an encrypted composition with the KDM from the store that is valid now, and Verify runs dcpdoctor's strict check as a job.
