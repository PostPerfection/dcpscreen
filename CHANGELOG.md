# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Settings can be locked with a password, so the player, display, sound and key settings, the library folders and the KDM store stay as the operator left them. While locked, Settings shows only a password field, and saving settings, adding or removing a KDM and removing a package from the library are refused. Unlocking lasts until Lock or the app exits, and a wrong password holds off the next attempt for 5 seconds. The password is stored as an Argon2id hash.
- Settings has a Player section for DCP playback: brightness, masks on each edge, scaling (fit, fill or native), the sound device and layout, an A/V delay, a subtitle offset and a subtitle colour override. They apply at startup and on Save, to a composition already playing too, and a missing sound device or a layout the device lacks shows on the status bar while it holds.
- GPU decoding: Settings has the grok accelerator plugin switch, licence and registration URL the wizards have. The setting applies at startup and on save, and a plugin that refuses to start turns it off with the reason in the status bar.
- Playback opens a player window of its own, as a normal window, while the transport and the library stay on the main window. Moving the pointer over the picture shows controls over it: play/pause, the scrubber with time, Stop and full screen. They fade out while playing, taking the pointer with them in full screen, and stay while paused or hovered. F, a double click or the full screen button go full screen on the display chosen in Settings, Escape leaves it, Space pauses, and Stop or closing the player window ends playback.
- Library, Keys, Jobs and Settings views: packages found under the library folders play in the player window, an encrypted composition with the KDM from the store that is valid now, and Verify runs dcpdoctor's strict check as a job.
